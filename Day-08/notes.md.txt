# Day 8 - RIGHT JOIN Notes

## What is RIGHT JOIN?

RIGHT JOIN returns:
- all records from the RIGHT table
- matching records from the LEFT table

If no match exists in the left table, NULL values are returned.

Syntax:

SELECT columns
FROM table1
RIGHT JOIN table2
ON table1.column = table2.column;

---

## Tables Used

Employees table:
Contains employee details.

Departments table:
Contains department details.

Departments is the RIGHT table in our queries.

---

## Queries Practiced

### 1. Basic RIGHT JOIN
Purpose:
Shows every department, even if no employee belongs to that department.

Learning:
Marketing appears even though no employee is assigned.

---

### 2. Departments without employees
Purpose:
Find departments that currently have no employees.

Logic:
If employee ID becomes NULL after join, it means no match was found.

---

### 3. Employee + Department details
Purpose:
Shows combined information from both tables.

Learning:
Useful when building reports.

---

### 4. RIGHT JOIN with filtering
Purpose:
Filter joined data for a specific department.

Learning:
RIGHT JOIN works normally with WHERE clauses.

---

### 5. Count employees per department
Purpose:
Count how many employees exist in each department.

Learning:
Departments with no employees show count as 0.

---

## Key Difference

INNER JOIN:
Only matching rows

LEFT JOIN:
Everything from left table

RIGHT JOIN:
Everything from right table

FULL JOIN:
Everything from both tables
