# SQL Day 18 - DATENAME(), DATETRUNC(), EOMONTH()

## DATENAME() vs DATEPART()

These two functions extract parts from dates.

However they return different data types.

---

## DATEPART()

Returns an INTEGER.

Syntax:

DATEPART(part, date)

Example:

SELECT DATEPART(MONTH, GETDATE());

Output:

5

Use Cases:
- Grouping
- Sorting
- Filtering
- Numeric calculations

Examples:

DATEPART(YEAR, GETDATE())
DATEPART(MONTH, GETDATE())
DATEPART(DAY, GETDATE())

---

## DATENAME()

Returns a STRING.

Syntax:

DATENAME(part, date)

Example:

SELECT DATENAME(MONTH, GETDATE());

Output:

May

Examples:

DATENAME(MONTH, GETDATE())
DATENAME(WEEKDAY, GETDATE())

Possible Outputs:

May
January
Monday
Friday

Use Cases:
- Reports
- Dashboards
- Human-readable outputs

---

## Difference

DATEPART(MONTH, GETDATE())

Output:

5

Datatype:

Integer

---------------------------------

DATENAME(MONTH, GETDATE())

Output:

May

Datatype:

String

---------------------------------

DATEPART = Number

DATENAME = Text

---

# DATETRUNC()

Introduced in SQL Server 2022.

Purpose:

Move a date to the beginning of a date period.

Syntax:

DATETRUNC(period, date)

Examples:

DATETRUNC(MONTH, GETDATE())

Output:

2026-05-01

---

DATETRUNC(YEAR, GETDATE())

Output:

2026-01-01

---

DATETRUNC(QUARTER, GETDATE())

Output:

2026-04-01

---

Use Cases:

- Monthly reports
- Quarterly reports
- Time series analysis
- Dashboard filtering

---

# EOMONTH()

Purpose:

Returns the last day of a month.

Syntax:

EOMONTH(date)

Example:

EOMONTH(GETDATE())

Output:

2026-05-31

---

Next Month End

EOMONTH(GETDATE(), 1)

Output:

2026-06-30

---

Use Cases

- Payroll calculations
- Billing cycles
- Monthly closing reports
- Subscription systems

---

# First Day vs Last Day

Beginning of month:

DATETRUNC(MONTH, GETDATE())

Output:

2026-05-01

End of month:

EOMONTH(GETDATE())

Output:

2026-05-31

---

# Important Interview Point

To get:

First Day of Month

Use:

DATETRUNC(MONTH, date)

To get:

Last Day of Month

Use:

EOMONTH(date)

Both are heavily used in business reporting systems.
